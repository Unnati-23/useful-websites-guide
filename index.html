Pro Tips

How to Actually Use These

Don't Hoard

Pick One, Not All Five

Saving resources feels productive, using them is what changes things. Choose the one that fixes your biggest problem right now and give it 20 minutes a day for two weeks.

SWAYAM

Check the Course Dates

Many SWAYAM courses run in semesters with enrolment windows. Learning is free; if you want the certificate, register for the proctored exam before the deadline.

Fitness Wiki

Start With the Guided Tour

The wiki is huge. The Guided Tour page lists the 3–5 best pages for your exact goal, whether that's losing fat, gaining weight or getting stronger.

Confidence

Pair Skillopedia With Practice

Watch one video, then use that one tip in a real conversation the same day. Combine it with British Council speaking practice and you'll feel the difference fast.
